⚖️ Indian Penal Code & RPA 1951

Your Legal Rights as a Voter

If you were proxied, denied your vote, or faced booth capturing — these are the laws that protect you. Know them, use them.

⚠️ What is "Proxy Voting" / Booth Capturing? — When someone casts your vote without your consent, prevents you from voting by force or intimidation, or impersonates you at a polling booth. This is a serious criminal offence under Indian law.

🚨 What To Do If You Were Proxied

Follow these steps immediately to protect your rights.

📸

1. Collect Evidence

Take photos, note witness names, record video if possible. Note the booth number and time.

🏛️

2. Report to Presiding Officer

Immediately report to the Presiding Officer at the booth. Demand a complaint be recorded.

📞

3. Call National Voter Helpline

Dial 1950 (24x7 during elections). Register your complaint with your voter ID number.

👮

4. File Police FIR

File an FIR under IPC Section 171D (personation) and 171C (undue influence) at the nearest police station.

📋

5. File ECI Complaint

File a complaint at eci.gov.in or through the cVIGIL app with location, photo, and description of violation.

⚖️

6. Seek Legal Aid

Contact your State Legal Services Authority (SLSA) for free legal representation if needed.
